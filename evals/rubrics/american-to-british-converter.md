# American to British English Converter Rubric

Score each criterion **PASS (1)** / **FAIL (0)** / **N/A**.
N/A criteria are excluded from the denominator.
Final score = sum(PASS) / sum(applicable) × 100.

---

## 1. Spelling & Vocabulary
*   **1.1 Spelling Conversion:** Converts US spellings (e.g., `-ize` to `-ise`, double L in traveler, `color` to `colour`) to UK spelling.
*   **1.2 Vocabulary Conversion:** Replaces US terms (e.g. `elevator`, `apartment`) with UK equivalents (`lift`, `flat`).

## 2. Case & Exception Handling
*   **2.1 Case Preservation:** Preserves casing (ALL CAPS, Title Case, lowercase) of modified words.
*   **2.2 Exception Handling:** Does not modify standard exceptions (e.g. `size` must remain `size`, `seize` must remain `seize`).

## 3. Measurements
*   **3.1 Customary Units:** Converts US customary units (Fahrenheit, pounds, inches, feet) to metric/UK equivalents accurately.
